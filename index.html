Frequently asked questions

What to expect when you commission a work, from the first message to the final handover.

How does a project begin?

We talk through what you want to make, who will use it and what must work on the first day. I then send back a plain written scope and a schedule for your approval before any work starts.

What do you build with?

I choose tools to fit the job: React and Next.js for the web, Flutter or Kotlin for apps, and Node with PostgreSQL or Firebase behind them. You are never tied to anything obscure.

Do you handle hosting and launch?

Yes. I set up hosting, the domain and the store listings, and when the work is done I hand over every login together with a short written guide.

Can you improve something that already exists?

Yes. I can review an existing site or app, fix what is slow or broken, and add new features without rewriting what already works.
